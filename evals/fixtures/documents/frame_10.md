# Frame 10

Tribute to my Grandfather. When you are young, you think life is endless and everything will stay the way it is, but things change and people evolve.

In the story, a soldier was fighting with his comrades to take control of a hill. He got stuck in a hole and questioned whether he should take a shot. He eventually came out of the hole, had tea with his comrades, and told the story for the next 60-70 years.

The broader reflection treats the war anecdote as part of family memory, nostalgia, and the way inherited stories shape how people understand their lives.
