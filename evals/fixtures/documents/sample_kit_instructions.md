# Sample Kit Instructions

This document is an instruction sheet for collecting and mailing sample bottles to a laboratory.

It describes how to label bottles, avoid touching the inside of caps, keep samples cold, complete the chain-of-custody form, and mail the kit before the pickup deadline.

The document explains that samples may be rejected if bottles are mislabeled, forms are incomplete, or shipping deadlines are missed.

The document does not report an emergency, outbreak, diagnosis, contamination event, or confirmed harm to any person.
